When the portfolio's visitor counter is enabled, it stores visit time, apparent IP address, an approximate country, and the device label and browser user-agent string in a private Supabase table. It doesn't use cookies or request GPS location. The country estimate can reflect a VPN or network exit rather than where a visitor is. The counter skips browsers that send Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

My portfolio and Mortgage Ops Lab have separate visitor counters. [IP geolocation data by DB-IP](https://db-ip.com/), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
